Key ERP constraints
Inventory pull timing
Inventory must pull from HCAR to field at time of sales order — not time of invoice. ERP must support this.
Barcode / serialization
Not serialized today, but future-proofing is required. ERP must support barcode scanning and future serialization.
Custom pricing per customer
Construct or itemized pricing, unique per customer, with ability to change price files. Must be native to ERP.
Royalty & commission reports
Quarterly royalty reports by SKU (excluding surgeon's own usage). Variable commissions per distributor with accruals.
