Current State · Inventory
Inventory Deep Dive
Forma's inventory is fundamentally non-standard. Six structural constraints make off-the-shelf ERP logic inadequate. Every system and integration decision flows from these constraints.
"These kits that we deliver to the hospital are extremely expensive and managing them is very, very critical." — Andrew Davison. Inventory continuity is the core operational constraint. No automation changes to inventory flows until the ERP decision is confirmed and client sign-off is received.
Why Forma's Inventory Is Non-Standard
Constraint 1
Variable Bill of Materials
Tray configurations are not fixed. Each tray is assembled with a variable BOM. Most ERPs assume fixed BOMs per SKU — will fail without explicit configuration.
Constraint 2
Consignment Model
Inventory deploys to the field before any sale occurs. The sale trigger (case sheet) happens post-surgery — potentially weeks after the inventory left the warehouse.
Constraint 3
Multi-Site Asset Tracking
HCAR warehouse → Field → Hospital → Return. Millions in assets tracked across multiple locations simultaneously, with no single system owning the full picture.
Constraint 4 — Platform Unknown
Tray Locator Outside ERP
Physical tray tracking runs in a completely separate system. Platform unidentified. Cannot be integrated until confirmed. This is a critical gap in the BI architecture.
Constraint 5
No Serialization (Yet)
Inventory is not currently serialized. Lot numbers and expiration date tracking status is unconfirmed. Serialization is a future-state requirement. Compliance implications need to be validated.
Constraint 6 — By Design
QMS / ERP Split
QMS records receipt and inspection (FDA-required). ERP records financial movement. These systems are permanently separate — quality records and financial records never share data. Do not disrupt.
