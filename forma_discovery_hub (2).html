Governing principles

Architecture before automation

No workflows or automation are implemented until current-state systems and processes are documented and validated.

ERP decision gate

No ERP-layer automation until the client confirms the future direction: Odoo OR QuickBooks Online + Implantbase.

Human approval gates

Major lifecycle transitions must include human review and sign-off before changes are executed.

AI assists — humans decide

AI provides analysis, recommendations, and insights. Final operational decisions remain human-driven.

Inventory process safeguards

Inventory process changes require client validation due to FDA medical device compliance requirements.
