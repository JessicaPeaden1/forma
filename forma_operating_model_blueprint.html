CRITICAL FLAGS — CURRENT STATE
⚠ Gmail is the order-to-cash trigger Case sheet email starts the entire order-to-cash process. Andrew rated email as "marginally important" — but it is structurally the intake gate for billing. A missed or delayed email directly delays invoicing.
⚠ QMS and ERP are completely siloed QMS records receiving inspections for FDA compliance. ERP records financial inventory. The two systems share zero data. Quality records from receiving inspections never reach financial records.
⚠ Tray Locator and Surgery Tracking platforms are unknown Tray location tracking exists as a separate system with no ERP integration. The surgery tracking platform has also not been identified. Both must be resolved before any inventory automation can be designed.
⚠ Rep compliance is structurally unreliable 45 independent distributors managed by 2 internal staff. Andrew: "boots on the ground compliance is absolutely terrible." Case sheet delays directly delay billing. No system enforcement exists.
⚑ Inventory must pull at Sales Order — not invoice This is a non-negotiable constraint. Whatever ERP is selected (Odoo or QB Online + Implantbase), inventory deduction must trigger at Sales Order creation — not at invoice. This must be preserved in the migration.
⚑ No ERP-layer automation until decision is confirmed Client is evaluating Odoo vs. QB Online + Implantbase. NetSuite was rejected ($150K+ customization). No automations should be designed at the ERP layer until the ERP decision is formally confirmed by Andrew.
⚑ No integrations exist between any core systems Andrew confirmed: "Highly disconnected. For sure." QB Desktop, Google Sheets, Dropbox, Tray Locator, QMS, and Gmail all operate as completely independent silos with no data flow between them.
⚑ Weekly AI strategy review is fully manual Andrew manually constructs prompts and feeds P&L, CRM, and surgery data into Grok, Gemini, and Claude weekly. Same inputs, same questions, every week. High-effort repeatable process — prime automation candidate.
AUTOMATION SIGNALS FROM DISCOVERY
Weekly data → AI aggregation Same inputs, same questions every week. Immediate automation candidate once the BI layer is defined and the ERP decision is confirmed.
Rep activity → Google Sheets 2 humans manually transcribing 45 independent reps weekly. High-risk single point of failure for CRM data quality and continuity.
Kit request via text / phone Zero system record. No acknowledgment sent to rep after request. High-value consignment assets shipped with no audit trail on either side.
Multi-model AI decision workflow Andrew runs the same decision through Grok, Gemini, and Claude in parallel manually. Prompt construction is cumbersome — templatable and triggerable once formalized.
Weekly cohort & sales tracking Defined metrics + defined targets = ideal automated dashboard candidate. No custom logic required — just automated pull from existing sources.
Dropbox — unindexed strategic asset Highly organized file tree, no search or AI access. A prior acquirer cited this organizational structure as a major exit asset. Currently completely dark to AI systems.
