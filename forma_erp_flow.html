ERP Requirements — Must Be Preserved in Any Migration
Inventory pull at Sales Order
Not at invoice time. Non-standard. Must be explicitly configured in any new ERP.
Variable Bill of Materials
Tray contents vary per build. Not fixed BOM. Breaks standard ERP logic — must be custom handled.
Customer-specific pricing
Construct or itemized. Unique per customer. Ability to update price files per account.
Royalty reports
SKU-based, quarterly. Must exclude designing surgeon's own usage from calculation.
Commission reports
Variable per distributor. Includes accruals and bonus incentives.
Barcode / scannable
Not in use today. ERP must support it — future-proof requirement.
Serialization readiness
Not serialized now. Must be able to activate in future without ERP rebuild.
SKU reorder analysis
Based on historical velocity + forecasted usage. Must be native or AI-assistable.
