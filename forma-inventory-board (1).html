Frame 1 — Why Non-Standard
Forma's Inventory Is Fundamentally Different
Six structural constraints that make standard ERPs inadequate. Every system decision flows from these.
"These kits that we deliver to the hospital are extremely expensive and managing them is very, very critical." — Andrew (Forma Medical)
Constraint 1
Variable Bill of Materials
Tray configurations are not fixed. Each tray is assembled with a variable BOM — most ERPs assume fixed BOMs per SKU.
Constraint 2
Consignment Model
Inventory deploys to the field before a sale occurs. The sale trigger (case sheet) happens post-surgery — potentially weeks later.
Constraint 3
Multi-Site Asset Tracking
HCAR warehouse → Field → Hospital → Return. Millions in assets simultaneously tracked across multiple locations.
Constraint 4
Tray Locator Outside ERP
Physical tray location is tracked in a separate system outside the ERP. Platform is currently Unverified
Constraint 5
No Serialization (Yet)
Inventory is not currently serialized. Serialization is a future-proofing requirement. Lot/expiration tracking status unconfirmed.
Constraint 6
QMS / ERP Split
QMS records receipt & inspection (FDA-required). ERP records financial movement. These systems are permanently separate by design.
Key Timing Constraint
⚠ Critical Accounting Rule
Inventory must pull from HCAR to Field at Sales Order creation — NOT at invoice time. Any future ERP must preserve this behavior. The timing gap between inventory movement and financial billing is a defining characteristic of the consignment model.
Royalty Traceability
Contractual Obligation
SKU-level usage must be tracked and reported quarterly. The designing surgeon's own usage must be excluded. This is a contractual and compliance obligation requiring granular inventory attribution.

The structural constraints that make Forma's inventory non-standard, and the unknowns that must be resolved.
Structural Constraints
📦
Consignment Model
Inventory deploys to field before sale occurs. Sale happens after product is already deployed. Warehouse → Field → Surgery → Invoice sequence.
🔧
Variable Tray Configuration
Trays use variable BOMs — not fixed. Most ERPs struggle to handle this. Critical for system selection.
⚡
Inventory Accounting Rule
Inventory must pull from HCAR at Sales Order creation — not at invoice. This is non-negotiable and must be preserved.
🗂️
Systems Are Disconnected
Data lives across ERP, QMS, Tray Locator, Email, and manual processes. No integration, no single source of truth.
